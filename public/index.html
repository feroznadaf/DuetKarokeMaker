Timestamp & Color Tag Syntax Guide

The app automatically parses both bracket and comma timestamp formats, along with singer color tags:

Format 1: Bracket Format (Recommended)

[1] [00:04.50 - 00:09.20] Singer 1 sings this phrase
[2] [00:09.80 - 00:15.00] Singer 2 responds here
[3] [00:16.00 - 00:22.50] Both sing together in unison

Format 2: Comma Timestamp Format

00:00:24.000,00:00:26.560
[2] Female singing line on the next line

Color Tags:

  • [1]: Assigns Singer 1 color (Default: Cyan #00FFFF)
  • [2]: Assigns Singer 2 color (Default: Magenta #FF00FF)
  • [3]: Assigns Duet/Both color (Default: Yellow #FFFF00)
